## The ACP Protocol & Zed Editor

**Speaker: [Benedikt Terhechte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benediktterhechte/)**

Benedikt took us on a journey through the evolution of AI coding tools: from the ChatGPT website with copy-paste, to Cursor, to the Claude and Codex CLIs. The ACP protocol promises to be the next step in this evolution. [View the slides](https://speakerdeck.com/terhechte/back-to-the-future-let-me-tell-you-about-the-acp-protocol).
