We're excited to announce **Hendrik Mans** as a speaker at [Agentic Conf Hamburg](https://lu.ma/45lfeyeh) on March 22nd!

## About Hendrik

Hendrik Mans has been building software for three decades. That's at least four too many.

## The Talk: Woop Coding!

It's time to face the shaming, ditch the vibing, and do some real freaking development together with your robot friend as an extension of yourself, not a servant.

Hendrik calls it Woop Coding: looks like Vibe Coding to the untrained eye, but with reliably great results, because you're smart, you're experienced, you're probably also beautiful, and you've learned how to use all this to turn your agent from a random result emitting automaton into your own personal power armor.

Now you're unbeatable. Now you're Woop Coding. Let's do this.
