We're proud to have **adesso insurance solutions** as a sponsor of [Agentic Conf Hamburg](https://agentic.hamburg) on March 22nd!

## About adesso insurance solutions

adesso insurance solutions, a subsidiary of adesso SE, is one of the leading providers of standard software for the insurance industry. With a team of 1,000 experts from insurance and IT, they develop the **in|sure Ecosphere** — a comprehensive, cloud-capable application landscape for the international insurance market. Their platform covers everything from portfolio and claims management to commission settlement, partner management, and company pension schemes. Beyond standard software, they also provide consulting and project support from conception to implementation.

## Why They Support Agentic Conf

With AI and automation reshaping how software gets built and maintained, adesso insurance solutions sees agentic engineering as a key driver for the next generation of insurance technology. By supporting Agentic Conf Hamburg, they're investing in the community that's defining how we build software in the age of AI.
