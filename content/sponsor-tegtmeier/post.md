We're proud to have **Tegtmeier Internet Solutions** as a sponsor of [Agentic Conf Hamburg](https://agentic.hamburg) on March 22nd!

## About Tegtmeier Internet Solutions

Hamburg-based Tegtmeier Internet Solutions has been solving digital challenges for businesses for over 20 years — whether strategy, processes, or custom software. As a full-service provider for consulting, development, and operations of internet solutions, they now also leverage AI to take business models to the next level. In 2024, they were recognized as a Great Place to Work.

## Why They Support Agentic Conf

Tegtmeier has been building custom software solutions for two decades, and they're now actively leveraging AI to accelerate their work. Supporting Agentic Conf Hamburg is a natural extension of their commitment to innovation and their belief in the power of community-driven learning.
