What a day.

**Agentic Conf Hamburg 2026** is in the books, and we're still buzzing from the energy, the conversations, and the community that showed up today.

## Thank You to Our Attendees

Over 250 of you came out – engineers, product managers, founders, students, and curious minds from across Germany and beyond. You brought the energy, the questions, and the conversations that made today special. A conference is nothing without the people in the room, and you made it unforgettable.

## Thank You to Our Speakers

To our 25+ speakers and workshop leaders: thank you for sharing your knowledge, your stories, and your hard-won lessons. You didn't give polished marketing talks – you shared real experiences from real projects. That's exactly what this community is about.

## Thank You to Our Sponsors

None of this would have been possible without our incredible sponsors:

- **Vorwerk** – for believing in the intersection of technology and tradition
- **adesso insurance solutions** – for investing in the future of insurance tech
- **Auto Fleet Control (AFC)** – for championing automation in fleet management
- **INNOQ** – for supporting the community that pushes technology forward
- **hackers&wizards** – for helping engineering teams adopt AI with structure
- **Digitale Leute School** – for bridging education and the AI era
- **Tegtmeier Internet Solutions** – for two decades of digital innovation, now powered by AI

Your support made it possible for us to bring this community together. Thank you.

## Thank You to Our Volunteers

Behind every smooth-running conference is a team of people who showed up early, stayed late, and handled a thousand small things that nobody sees. To our volunteers: you are the backbone of this event. We couldn't have done it without you.

## What's Next

This was the first Agentic Conf Hamburg – but it won't be the last. The conversations that started today will keep going in our regular meetups, in our community channels, and in the work we all do every day.

If you took photos, wrote notes, or had a breakthrough moment today – share it! Tag us on LinkedIn or use the hashtag **#AgenticConf**. We'd love to see the day through your eyes.

Until next time, Hamburg. It's been an honor.
