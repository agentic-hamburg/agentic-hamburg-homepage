It's here – **Agentic Conf Hamburg 2026** is officially underway!

Right now, over 250 engineers, product builders, and AI enthusiasts are gathering in Hamburg for a full day of talks, workshops, and conversations about the future of agentic software engineering.

## What's Happening Today

The energy in the room is incredible. Our lineup of 25+ speakers and workshop leaders are sharing real-world experiences with AI coding tools, agentic workflows, and the new ways software gets built when humans and AI collaborate.

From lightning talks to deep-dive workshops, from enterprise adoption stories to hands-on coding sessions – today is all about learning from practitioners who are doing the work, not just talking about it.

## The Talks

Our speakers are covering everything from scaling LLM expertise across engineering teams to sandboxing AI-generated code, from product discovery in the AI era to building autonomous software delivery pipelines. Every talk comes from real experience, real projects, and real lessons learned.

## The Workshops

Hands-on sessions are running throughout the day, giving attendees the chance to get their hands dirty with agentic tools and techniques they can take back to their teams on Monday.

## Follow Along

Can't be here in person? Follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/agentic-hamburg) for live updates throughout the day. We'll be sharing highlights, key takeaways, and behind-the-scenes moments.

And if you're here – enjoy the day, meet new people, and don't forget to grab some coffee between sessions!
