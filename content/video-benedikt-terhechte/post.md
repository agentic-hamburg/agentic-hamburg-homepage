We're happy to share the video of **Benedikt Terhechte's** talk from [Agentic Conf Hamburg 2026](https://agentic.hamburg)!

## The Talk: Humans Still Needed: Learnings from Refactoring an Ancient Codebase

AI agents have become fantastic at green field projects – but what happens when you point them at a complex, existing codebase? Benedikt shared his experience porting a 100k LoC Obj-C/OpenGL iOS game from 2011 to modern iOS with the help of Claude Opus.

It required much more than a simple prompt. The agent lacked the creativity to solve certain tasks, was often overwhelmed by the sheer scope, and some problems required a fundamental change of abstraction. Benedikt walked us through the initial setup, how he improved it during the project, what the agent struggled with, and how he was able to guide it to success.

A refreshingly honest look at where AI coding shines – and where humans are still very much needed.
