**Agentic Coding Meetup #8 is coming on October 13.** Join us at Haiilo in Hamburg for an evening of talks and conversations about building software with coding agents.

## First Talk Announced: Calvin Dudek

**Calvin Dudek, CPTO at OMR Reviews**, will share what his team learned on the way to becoming AI-native — beyond simply buying better tools.

His talk, **“Going AI-Native Costs More Than a License,”** covers concrete lessons on AI-ready codebases, changing workflows, and what happens when the bottleneck is no longer writing code.

More talks will be announced soon.
